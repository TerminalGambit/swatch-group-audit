# The Operating Leverage Story

### Why a 14.6% Revenue Fall Became a 74.5% Profit Collapse

**Fixed cost base: ~CHF 3,945m (58.6% of 2024 net sales)**

| Fixed / Semi-Fixed Cost | 2024 (CHF m) | % of Sales | YoY Change |
|------------------------|-------------:|-----------:|-----------:|
| Personnel expense | 2,506 | 37.2% | -1.7% |
| Maintenance, rents & energy | 1,023 | 15.2% | -3.2% |
| Depreciation & amortization | 416 | 6.2% | +6.4% |
| **Total fixed/semi-fixed** | **3,945** | **58.6%** | **-1.5%** |

- Personnel fell only CHF 44m on a CHF 1,153m revenue decline
- Swiss labor law + Hayek family employment commitment = minimal flex
- Retail lease costs (CHF 551m) contractually non-cancellable
- **Operating leverage multiplier: 5.1x** — every 1% revenue decline = 5.1% profit decline

# Macro: China Structural vs. Cyclical

### The 60/40 Diagnosis

| Factor | Assessment | Evidence |
|--------|:----------:|---------|
| China property wealth -20% from peak | Cyclical | Government stimulus begun; 2–4 year recovery typical |
| Youth unemployment 18.8% peak | Cyclical | Eases with economic recovery |
| "Luxury shame" / anti-ostentation | Mixed | Similar 2012–2014 episode recovered in 2–3 years |
| Mid-market secular squeeze | Structural | Pre-owned + smartwatch competition accelerating |
| Market share loss -200 bps | Mixed | Partly China, partly brand positioning |
| Ultra-luxury polarization | Structural | Big Four concentration accelerating for 5+ years |

**Swiss watch industry exports to China: -25.8%** — "worse than the COVID pandemic" (FH)
Chinese luxury spending fell 18–20% onshore; 40% of spending redirected to international travel
Japan record spending driven by CHF/JPY — tourist luxury redistribution, not structural

**Probability: 60% cyclical, 40% structural — recovery will be partial, not full restoration**

# Conclusion: 3 Findings, 3 Actions

### Three Definitive Audit Findings

**1. Operating leverage is the structural mechanism:** 14.6% revenue decline → 74.5% profit decline. Fixed costs of CHF 3.9B (59% of sales) cannot flex quickly. This is strategy, not failure.

**2. Two financial quality risks require monitoring:** ETR spike (36.5%, CHF 863m unrecognized loss pool) and inventory concentration (CHF 7.6B, 54.6% of assets, NRV risk if recovery delayed).

**3. Below-book entry with China recovery optionality:** P/B 0.70–0.95x with CHF 12.2B equity backed by tangible assets and zero financial distress risk. Earnings recovery is a cyclical bet on China + operating leverage in reverse.

### Three Recommended Actions

**1. Monitor Greater China quarterly → recalibrate inventory write-down risk** if H1 2025 shows no recovery trajectory

**2. Demand ETR transparency** — require disclosure of jurisdictions driving non-recognition; the CHF 863m carryforward pool is a material contingency

**3. Watch Electronic Systems order-to-revenue conversion** — a +25% order book growth is the clearest near-term fundamental positive signal in the entire report
