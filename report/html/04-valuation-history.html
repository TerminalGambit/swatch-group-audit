Revenue Gap: FY2024 Still 18.3% Below 2019 Peak — Six Years On
FY2024 net sales of CHF 6,735M represent an CHF 1,508M shortfall versus the FY2019 peak of CHF 8,243M. Despite record operating margins in 2022–2023, the Group never recovered nominal revenue to pre-pandemic levels. In real terms — accounting for CHF appreciation and Swiss inflation — the gap is materially larger.
Amplified Downturns: Revenue Decline Translates to Catastrophic Operating Decline
The Group's fixed-cost structure (Swiss manufacturing, owned retail) creates extreme operating leverage. In 2020, a -32% revenue decline produced a -94.9% operating result collapse (CHF 1,023M to CHF 52M). In 2024, a -14.6% revenue decline produced a -74.5% operating result collapse (CHF 1,191M to CHF 304M). The same structural dynamic that enabled 15.4% margins in 2022 becomes a liability when volumes contract.

2024 Shows 5.1x Negative Operating Leverage — The Core Structural Risk
For every 1% revenue decline in 2024, operating profit fell 5.1%. This is a direct consequence of Swatch Group's vertical integration model: Swiss manufacturing capacity, owned retail networks, and an in-house production division cannot be scaled down quickly. When volumes recover, this same structure generates exceptional margins (15.4% in 2022). But the downside is symmetric and severe. The Production division — supplying Swiss Made components to third parties — amplifies this effect: it has high fixed costs and saw revenue contract as the entire Swiss watch industry destocked.

Governance — Hayek Family Control Structure

Dual-Class Structure: 25% Economic Interest, 44.1% Voting Control

The Hayek Pool controls 44.1% of voting rights (as of December 31, 2024) via the registered share concentration permitted under Swatch Group's dual-class structure. Registered shares carry the same one vote as bearer shares but trade at 1/5 the price (CHF 41.80 vs. CHF 211.70), giving the family approximately ~25% economic interest while commanding a blocking minority on all shareholder votes.

The 2025 activist board challenge reflects growing minority shareholder frustration with the Group's operational performance and capital allocation during the 2024 downturn. Buybacks continuing during the earnings collapse paradoxically serve family interests: they shrink public float, which mechanically increases the Hayek family's relative voting weight without any direct family investment. The July 2024 family purchase of CHF 14.3M in bearer shares at market lows was a confidence signal — and also extended voting control to 44.1%.

Section 10

Auditor's Valuation Conclusion

Valuation Assessment — May 2026

At CHF 211.70 (bearer), Swatch Group trades at 0.95x book value — historically unusual for a luxury goods company and a signal that the market is pricing in continued earnings pressure rather than near-term recovery. The 49.6x P/E on FY2024 earnings is not the operative metric; the FY2024 EPS of CHF 3.73 reflects an exceptional earnings trough driven by China demand collapse and Production division deleveraging, not structural impairment of the business.

The more relevant entry-point multiples are EV/Sales 1.5x and EV/EBITDA 14–17x, which compare reasonably against Swiss luxury peers on a trough-earnings basis. On a normalized earnings assumption (return to 10–12% operating margins, consistent with the 2019–2023 range), the forward P/E compresses substantially.

The 30% premium to analyst consensus implies the market is pricing a faster China recovery than sell-side models assume — or that recent analyst upgrades (BofA, RBC, Citi) are still lagging a fundamental re-rating already underway. The stock's recovery from CHF 127 lows to CHF 211.70 (+67%) validates that the market saw the trough ahead of consensus.

Key monitoring metrics for audit follow-through: (1) China luxury goods data Q1–Q2 2025 — any inflection in Mainland Chinese spending; (2) Watches & Jewelry segment operating margin trajectory — recovery from 10.6% toward 15%+ would be decisive; (3) Production division operating result — the segment that amplifies downturns will also amplify the recovery; (4) inventory days outstanding — elevated finished goods inventory signals channel saturation has not cleared; (5) whether management initiates formal financial guidance — currently absent, which itself signals management uncertainty.
