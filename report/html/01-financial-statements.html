Effective Tax Rate Spike — Note 27

ETR surged from 22.7% (2023) to 36.5% (2024), against a Biel HQ applicable rate of 20.9%. The primary driver: CHF 41M additional tax burden from non-recognition of CHF 863M in unrecognized loss carryforwards across 37 jurisdictions — predominantly in Greater China and loss-making European subsidiaries. The unrecognized carryforward pool grew from CHF 848M (2023) to CHF 952M total, of which only CHF 89M is recognized. Potential tax asset if utilized: CHF 152M.

Inventory Build Risk — Note 7

Inventories rose +CHF 332M (+4.5%) to CHF 7,641M despite net sales declining -14.6%. Finished goods alone grew from CHF 3,565M to CHF 3,918M (+CHF 353M). The Group is deliberately maintaining production capacity — a strategic choice — but Days Inventory Outstanding is rising sharply. If the Chinese consumer recovery is delayed into 2026, write-down risk on CHF 7.6B of inventory is material. PwC identified inventory valuation as the sole Key Audit Matter; additional write-downs in 2024 totalled CHF 47M (CHF 39M net). Inventories represent 54.6% of total assets.

Operating Lease Off-Balance-Sheet Exposure — Note 28

CHF 2,122M in total non-cancellable operating lease commitments (2023: CHF 1,958M), predominantly retail store contracts. Under IFRS 16 equivalent treatment, this would add approximately CHF 1.7–1.9B to reported debt and right-of-use assets — increasing leverage ratios significantly. Leasing costs recognized in 2024: CHF 551M. Additionally, other irrevocable commitments grew from CHF 502M (2023) to CHF 730M (+45.4%), mainly marketing, sponsoring, and capex commitments. Swiss GAAP FER does not require IFRS 16-style recognition; these commitments remain off-balance-sheet.

07Key Notes Highlights

Note 27 — Effective Tax Rate Spike (22.7% → 36.5%)

The 2024 effective tax rate of 36.5% is 15.6 percentage points above the applicable rate of 20.9% and a 13.8 percentage point increase from 2023. The primary driver is non-recognition of CHF 41M in loss carryforward deferred tax assets. Total unrecognized carryforward losses reached CHF 863M (2023: CHF 726M) — a growing pool reflecting loss-making subsidiaries across the Group's 37 tax jurisdictions. These losses have an estimated unrecognized deferred tax value of CHF 152M. BEPS 2.0 global minimum tax came into force 1 January 2024; additional top-up taxes were less than CHF 1M in 2024, but Irish and Middle East operations may face material impacts from 2025.

Note 7 — Inventory Risk: CHF 7,641M at 54.6% of Total Assets

Inventories remain the single largest balance sheet item and the sole key audit matter. Despite a -14.6% revenue decline, inventory grew +CHF 332M to CHF 7,641M. Finished goods make up CHF 3,918M (51.3% of total inventory). The Group wrote down CHF 47M of additional inventory in 2024 (net -CHF 39M after reversals), down slightly from CHF 53M in 2023. The deliberate capacity-maintenance strategy is coherent but heightens impairment risk. A China recovery delay of more than 12 months would likely require accelerated write-downs. PwC concluded management's approach is "reasonable" but this warrants ongoing monitoring. Days Inventory Outstanding is estimated to exceed 400 days based on 2024 COGS.

Note 3 — Unqualified Audit Opinion / Clean Post-Balance Sheet

PricewaterhouseCoopers AG (Basel) issued an unqualified opinion on the consolidated financial statements as at 31 December 2024, approved by the Board on 5 March 2025. Auditor in charge: Thomas Brüderlin and Tobias Handschin. Audit scope: 70 reporting units across 30 countries, covering more than 85% of Group net sales. Group materiality: CHF 40M. Parent company (Swatch Group Ltd) also received an unqualified opinion with CHF 35M materiality. Note 3 confirms no significant events occurred after the balance sheet date. The next shareholder approval milestone is the Annual General Meeting of 21 May 2025.
