## 7. Short Interest

Short interest data for the SIX Swiss Exchange-listed UHR shares is **not publicly available** through open web sources. Key points:
- The SIX Swiss Exchange does not publicly publish aggregated short positions in real time (unlike the US SEC/FINRA system)
- ORTEX tracks estimated short interest for BIT:1UHR (Italian ADR equivalent) but requires a subscription
- US OTC equivalents (SWGAF, SWGNF, SWGAY) have US-reported short interest but very low volume and are not representative
- **Recommendation:** Obtain short interest data from ORTEX or SIX's official short position reports (available via regulators under EU Short Selling Regulation equivalents)

## Key Themes for Audit

1. **Earnings quality crisis:** FY2024 net income CHF 193M (vs CHF 869M in FY2023), a 78% decline. FY2025 net income CHF 3M (98% decline from FY2024) — primarily due to an 84% effective tax rate (one-time). Makes year-over-year comparison unreliable.

2. **Dividend policy under stress:** Payout ratios exceeded 100% in both FY2020 and FY2024, meaning Swatch is maintaining dividends from reserves. This is a deliberate family-controlled capital allocation signal, not financially driven.

3. **Valuation re-rating:** Stock dropped to 16-year lows (~CHF 127–141) before recovering +50% in the following 12 months. Trading above analyst consensus targets as of May 2026.

4. **Family control concentration:** Hayek family has 44.1% of votes on ~25% of economic capital. An activist investor challenge in 2025 highlights corporate governance tension.

5. **Structural headwinds:** Chinese luxury demand weakness drove 2024 revenue decline of 12–14%. This is the primary macro risk for Swatch given heavy exposure to mid-range luxury (Omega, Longines) rather than ultra-high-end.
