## Note 2 — Basis of Preparation

**Accounting standard changes in 2024:**

1. **FER 28 Government Grants** (effective 1 January 2024) — no significant impact. CHF 2m of government grants included in Other operating income; related to Olympic Games timing services and income-related grants.

2. **FER 30 Consolidated Financial Statements** (revised, effective 1 January 2024) — requires:
   - Separate identification and recognition of intangible assets from acquisitions not previously recognized by the acquired subsidiary
   - Recycling of accumulated translation differences when a foreign entity is liquidated/closed (not just sold)
   - Accounting policies for Intangible assets (Note 9), Consolidation (Note 30), and Goodwill (Note 33) adjusted accordingly

**Key accounting estimates and judgments:**
- **Inventories:** Subject to market risk/obsolescence assessments; evaluated at lower of cost or net realizable value
- **Provisions:** Warranty provisions based on empirical repair/return volumes; restructuring provisions only recognized when legitimate third-party expectations exist
- **Income taxes:** Complex area given operations in 37 countries; BEPS 2.0 global minimum tax came into force 1 January 2024

- Testing Management's lower-of-cost-or-NRV valuation for finished goods

## Note 17 — Financial Risk Management

### a. Financial Risk Factors
The Group is exposed to:
- **Foreign currency risk** — primary focus: USD, CNY, EUR, JPY
- **Credit risk** — from customers and counterparties
- **Liquidity risk** — managed by Group Treasury

**FX Hedging Policy:**
- Group Treasury manages FX risk centrally following Group management directives
- May use derivative financial instruments: forward currency contracts and currency options
- Purpose: minimize impact of exchange rate fluctuations on net result
- Derivatives are NOT designated as cash flow hedges — all booked at fair value through P&L
- Currency counterparty risk minimized by using investment-grade financial institutions only

## Summary of Significant Accounting Policies

| Policy Area | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Revenue recognition | Transfer of significant risks and rewards; accruals for discounts/rebates |
| Inventories | Lower of cost or NRV; weighted average cost or standard cost |
| PPE | Historical cost less accumulated depreciation; straight-line |
| Intangible assets | Historical cost less accumulated amortization; straight-line (max 5 years development/software, max 20 years key money) |
| Goodwill | Charged directly to Group equity; not capitalized |
| Securities | Fair value through income statement |
| Derivatives | Fair value through income statement; no hedge accounting |
| Financial debts | Nominal value |
| Pension (Swiss) | Swiss GAF FER 26; economic benefit/obligation recognized |
| Pension (foreign) | Contribution basis or obligation recognized on balance sheet |
| Foreign currency | Transactions at transaction date rate; balance sheet items at year-end rate |
| Consolidation | Full consolidation >50% control; equity method 20-50% |
| Deferred taxes | Full balance sheet approach; tax rates enacted at balance sheet date |
| Provisions | Recognized when probable outflow and reliable estimate; no discounting on non-current provisions |
| Government grants | Asset-related: deferred in accrued expenses, released over asset life; Income-related: recognized with corresponding expense |
